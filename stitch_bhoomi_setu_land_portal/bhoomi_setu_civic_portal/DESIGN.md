## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an authoritative, high-integrity spatial governance platform designed for civic administrators, surveyors, and citizens navigating land acquisition and spatial records in Karnataka. The visual personality communicates absolute trustworthiness, administrative clarity, and modern civic infrastructure.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Minimalist Civic Structure** with refined institutional detailing:
- **Clean & Spacious Foundation**: Generous breathing room across complex data matrices, surveying records, and geospatial widgets to reduce cognitive fatigue in bureaucratic and legal workflows.
- **Institutional Weight with Modern Touch**: Grounded deep teal structures reinforced by crisp hairline boundaries, paired with warm administrative amber accents rather than utilitarian generic blues.
- **Bilingual Harmony**: Native optical parity across English and Kannada scripts, ensuring parity of legibility for legal disclaimers, status identifiers, and spatial records.
- **Dignified Seals & Motifs**: Subdued state crest styling and official seal geometry integrated seamlessly without visual clutter or ornamental excess.

## Colors
The palette evokes civic authority, legal permanence, and geographic stewardship through deep, organic mineral teals and high-contrast regulatory markers.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Canvas & Ground**: `#F7F7F5` serves as the institutional parchment ground, reducing eye strain in high-density data environments while maintaining warm, welcoming contrast.
- **Deep Teal Authority (Primary)**: `#0B4F4A` anchors all primary actions, top-level navigation banners, and dominant institutional framing. Hover and active states elevate into `#0F6B5C`.
- **Administrative Amber (Tertiary & Accent)**: `#F2B705` provides deliberate visual prioritization for urgent notices, critical plot verification actions, pending approvals, and bilingual statutory callouts.
- **Text & Contrast (Neutral)**: Primary reading content relies on deep charcoal `#1C1C1C` to ensure WCAG AAA compliance against both `#F7F7F5` and pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`). Muted secondary metadata rests at `#64748B`.
- **Structural Partition**: Hairline borders utilize slate `#E2E8F0` across containers, cards, and data matrices.

### Statutory & Spatial Risk System
- **Low Risk / Clear Title**: Surface `#ECFDF5`, Border `#A7F3D0`, Text `#065F46`, Accent Dot `#10B981`.
- **Medium Risk / Pending Gazette Verification**: Surface `#FFFBEB`, Border `#FDE68A`, Text `#92400E`, Accent Dot `#F59E0B`.
- **High Risk / Disputed or Forest Buffer Zone**: Surface `#FEF2F2`, Border `#FECACA`, Text `#991B1B`, Accent Dot `#EF4444`.

## Typography
The typographic architecture creates an unmistakable civic rhythm:
- **Display & Headings (Noto Serif)**: Leveraged for authoritative headers, modal proclamations, district names, and portal section banners. It introduces classical state prestige and permanence.
- **Body & Data Tables (Inter)**: High-density numeric survey numbers, RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops) tables, and legal parcels demand extreme clarity and neutral legibility across dense listings.
- **Bilingual & Institutional Badging (Noto Sans)**: Optimized for native Kannada glyph renders alongside English microcopy, ensuring identical baseline alignments in compound badges (e.g., `ಭೂ ಸ್ವಾಧೀನ / Land Acquisition`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a structured, highly predictable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate GIS map canvas viewports alongside dense acquisition metadata sidebars.

### Spatial Grid Rules
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, max layout containment of `1408px` (88rem) centered on viewport. Sidebars for parcel tracking utilize a fixed 380px or 4-column span, while GIS spatial maps take the remaining 8 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 16px gutters, 32px external margins. GIS maps and parcel details switch to a stacked vertical split (40vh map, 60vh scrollable inspection panel).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margin. High-priority filters, verification search bars, and parcel cards convert to full single-column linear layout. Bottom floating bar handles state actions.

### Spacing Rhythms
Strict adherence to an 8px base grid ensures clean vertical metrics. Form layouts utilize 24px spacing between input groupings, while related micro-labels sit 6px directly above input boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects deep drop shadows in favor of crisp administrative surfaces, soft tactile ambient layers, and precise hairline borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Ground)**: `#F7F7F5` background canvas with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Standard Parcel Cards & Content Panels)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface container encapsulated by a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient diffuse shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 79, 74, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Survey Modules)**: `#FFFFFF` surface raised slightly with `0px 6px 18px rgba(11, 79, 74, 0.08)` and border transition to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (GIS Map Floating Tools & Action Drawers)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` with soft tinted lift: `0px 10px 28px rgba(11, 79, 74, 0.12), 0px 1px 2px rgba(11, 79, 74, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Statutory Modals & Legal Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` with focused backdrop curtain: `0px 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14)`. Backdrop curtain uses `#0B4F4A` at 35% opacity with 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes
To balance bureaucratic authority with approachable civic accessibility, the design system utilizes generous curves on external containers paired with refined structure internally:

- **Large Surfaces & Data Cards**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (16px) or `rounded-2xl` (24px) to present an open, citizen-first visual presence.
- **Buttons, Field Inputs, & Selects**: Consistent `rounded-lg` (8px to 10px) geometry to ensure structural rigor and precise spatial alignment in tabular displays.
- **Badges, Status Pills, & Coordinates Tags**: Full pill styling (`rounded-full`) to delineate them clearly from actionable structural buttons.
- **Map Tools & Floating Canvas Controls**: Rounded square framing (`rounded-xl`) with 1px slate boundaries.

- **Structural Spec**: Full pill format, 4px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding.
- **Kannada + English Display**: Flex row alignment with a 6px dividing bullet or subtle vertical divider. Text format: `ಕಡಿಮೆ ಅಪಾಯ | Low Risk`.
- **Low Risk Variant**: Background `#ECFDF5`, Border `#A7F3D0`, Text `#065F46`, Left 6px status orb in `#10B981`.
- **Medium Risk Variant**: Background `#FFFBEB`, Border `#FDE68A`, Text `#92400E`, Left 6px status orb in `#F59E0B`.
- **High Risk Variant**: Background `#FEF2F2`, Border `#FECACA`, Text `#991B1B`, Left 6px status orb in `#EF4444`.

### 3. Cards & Spatial Parcel Panels
- **Container**: `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl` (20px-24px), border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding 24px.
- **Card Header**: Displays district/taluk micro-label in Noto Sans above the primary parcel survey title in Noto Serif (e.g., `Survey No. 142/2A - Devanahalli`).
- **Footer Metadata Band**: Distinct `#F8FAFC` recessed sub-surface with top 1px border divider (`#E2E8F0`), hosting timestamp, verified stamp, and GIS coordinate triggers.

### 4. Form Inputs & Survey Lookups
- **Field Layout**: Background `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, Border Radius 8px, Text `#1C1C1C`.
- **Focus State**: 1px solid `#0B4F4A` with an ambient glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(11, 79, 74, 0.15)`.
- **Field Labels**: Inter Medium 13px in `#334155` accompanied by Kannada descriptor in Noto Sans 11px muted slate.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios, & Spatial Layer Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px dimensions, 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Selected fill `#0B4F4A` with crisp white checkmark or center dot.
- **Map Layer Switch (Toggle)**: Track `#E2E8F0`, Active Track `#0B4F4A`, Thumb `#FFFFFF` with 2px elevation.

### 6. Official Seal & Crest Styling
- **State Header Badge**: Embedded in navigation bar or card verification seals. Dual ring circular line geometry with `#0F6B5C` outline containing the stylized crest, backed by a warm `#FEFCE8` tint for certified state authentications.
